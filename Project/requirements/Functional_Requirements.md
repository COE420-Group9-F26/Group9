# Functional Requirements

## Individual Contributions

### Alan Dsouza

FR-A1

Customers should be able to view their prescription status.

FR-A2

Notifications should be delivered to the customer when the prescription status is changed.

FR-A3

It should allow inventory management by pharmacy admin, where they can add, edit, or remove medications, their stock, and their attributes.

FR-A4

The system should have role-based access via a login system, where customers, staff and admin can log into the system to access their relevant UI.

FR-A5

The customer should be able to register an account via self-service.


### Omar Alkhatib

FR-OA1

The system should allow authorized pharmacists to review an uploaded prescription and approve or reject it with a reason.

FR-OA2

The system should verify requested quantity is available before confirming an order and prevent ordering unavailable stocks.

FR-OA3

The system should allow an authorized pharmacist to request clarification from a customer regarding an unclear or incomplete prescription.

FR-OA4

The system should allow the pharmacy owner to create, update, and deactivate staff accounts and assign roles to them.

FR-OA5

The system should prevent an order from being confirmed when payment fails and allow the customer to retry the payment.


### Omar Abdalla

FR-OD1

It should associate each successfully uploaded prescription with the customer who submitted it and the corresponding order.

FR-OD2

It should prevent an order containing prescription-required medicine from proceeding to the payment stage until its associated prescription has been approved by an authorized pharmacist.

FR-OD3

It should allow a customer to cancel an order before the order enters the preparation stage.

FR-OD4

It should automatically recalculate the total price of the shopping cart whenever the customer changes the quantity of an item or removes an item.

FR-OD5

It should allow a logged-in customer to view a list of their previously placed orders, including the order date, total amount, and final order status.


### Qusai Al Tah

FR-Q1

The system shall allow customers to search for medicines by product name, category, or active ingredient.

FR-Q2

The system shall display the current price and stock availability of each medicine before the customer adds it to the shopping cart.

FR-Q3

The system shall allow customers to choose between delivery and pharmacy pickup during checkout.

FR-Q4

The system shall allow authorised pharmacy staff to update the preparation status of a confirmed order.

FR-Q5

The system shall notify the customer when their order is ready for pickup or has been dispatched for delivery.
