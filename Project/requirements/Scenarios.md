# Software System Scenarios

## S-01 - Success Scenario for Prescription

Contributor: Alan

Actor: Customer

A customer logs into the pharmacy website, submits a prescription, and waits. Upon the prescription status being approved, the customer is notified and opens the prescription on the website. Then, they will pay for the prescription online. The payment is completed and approved, and the prescription status is updated to indicate it is ready for collection or delivery. The customer arranges collection or delivery.

## S-02 - Purchase Non-prescriptive Medicine

Contributor: Omar Abdalla

Actor: Customer

A customer logs into the pharmacy website, searches for Panadol, views its price and availability, adds two boxes to the shopping cart, confirms the delivery information, completes payment, and receives confirmation that the order was created.

## S-03 - Check Prescription Status

Contributor: Omar Alkhatib

Actor: Customer

A customer logs into the pharmacy website, opens their prescriptions, selects a submitted prescription, views its current status, and receives a notification when the pharmacy updates it.

## S-04 - Order Pharmacy Pickup

Contributor: Qusai Al Tah

Actor: Customer

A customer logs into the pharmacy website, searches for a medicine, checks its price and availability, adds the medicine to the shopping cart, and proceeds to checkout. The customer selects pharmacy pickup as the fulfillment method, completes the payment, and receives confirmation that the order has been created. Once the pharmacy prepares the order, the customer receives a notification that it is ready for pickup.

## S-05 - Process Prescription Order

Contributor: Qusai Al Tah

Actor: Pharmacist

An authorized pharmacist logs into the pharmacy system and opens the list of submitted prescriptions. The pharmacist selects a prescription, reviews the uploaded document and related order, and approves the prescription if it is valid. The system updates the prescription status and allows the associated order to proceed. The pharmacist then updates the order preparation status, and the customer is notified when the order is ready for pickup or delivery.
