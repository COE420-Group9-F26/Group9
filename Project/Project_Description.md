Fulfilling medical prescriptions are typically a messy ordeal. A doctor must fill a prescription, give it to the patient, and the patient must give it to a pharmacist to retrieve their medication. Our system intends to streamline this process by allowing doctors to add prescription information into our software system, which is both immediately visible to the pharmacist so that they can keep the prescription ready for collection, and to the patient so they can see relevant information and track the status of the prescription. In this case, the prescription is an item with a finite life cycle that can be rejected or approved by the pharmacist (for example, rejected if the medication is not in stock or cannot be released). This rejection or approval will update the prescription's status and notify the doctor and patient.
