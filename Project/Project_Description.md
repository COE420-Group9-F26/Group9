La Roche Pharmacy Online Prescription and Medicine Management System is a web-based platform designed to digitise and manage the pharmacy's customer-facing and internal operations. Customers can browse available medicines and pharmacy products, create accounts, submit prescriptions, place online orders, make payments, and track their orders. Pharmacy staff can review prescriptions, process orders, update order status, manage inventory, and communicate with customers when necessary. The pharmacy owner has administrative access to manage staff accounts, medicines, inventory, pricing, orders, and system information. The system aims to create a structured digital pipeline from customer request to prescription verification, payment, preparation, and final order completion.
